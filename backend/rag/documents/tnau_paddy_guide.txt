# TNAU Agritech Portal: Paddy (Rice) Comprehensive Agronomic & Weather Management Guide
Source: TNAU Agritech Portal - Department of Agronomy, Tamil Nadu Agricultural University
Document ID: TNAU-AGRI-PADDY-2024-V1
Crop: Paddy (Oryza sativa)

## Section 1: Water Management & Irrigation Scheduling
- Critical Water Requirement Stages:
  1. Vegetative Stage (Tillering): Maintain shallow standing water depth of 2 to 3 cm.
  2. Panicle Initiation to Flowering (Booting & Heading): Highly sensitive stage. Maintain 5 cm standing water. Water stress at this stage causes high spikelet sterility.
  3. Grain Filling & Ripening Stage: Maintain saturation to thin water film (1-2 cm). Withhold irrigation 7 to 10 days before harvest to facilitate uniform grain drying and mechanical harvesting.
- Weather-Responsive Irrigation Rule:
  - If cumulative 3-day rainfall forecast exceeds 20 mm or past 3 days rainfall exceeds 30 mm, postpone irrigation and open drainage channels to prevent lodging.
  - If estimated soil moisture exceeds 0.35 m³/m³ (or saturation > 80%), avoid standing water inundation unless preparing nursery.
  - Alternating Wetting and Drying (AWD): In water-scarce conditions, irrigate to 5 cm depth only after water level drops to 15 cm below soil surface (observed using field water tube).

## Section 2: Pest and Disease Management & Chemical Spraying Rules
- Key Pests & Recommended Control:
  - Yellow Stem Borer: Economic Threshold Level (ETL) is 2 egg masses/m² or 10% dead hearts. Spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.3 ml/L (60 ml/acre) or Cartap Hydrochloride 50% SP @ 2 g/L (400 g/acre).
  - Rice Blast (Magnaporthe oryzae): ETL is 1% leaf area affected. Apply Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6 g/L (120 g/acre) or Azoxystrobin 25 SC @ 1 ml/L.
  - Brown Plant Hopper (BPH): Spray Pymetrozine 50% WDG @ 0.6 g/L. Avoid synthetic pyrethroids which cause pest resurgence.
- Strict Weather Spraying Guardrails:
  - Wind Speed Threshold: NEVER spray when wind speed exceeds 15 km/h to prevent spray drift and wastage.
  - Rainfall Threshold: Do NOT spray if rainfall is forecasted within the next 4 to 6 hours or if rainfall probability is greater than 50%.
  - Temperature & Humidity: Spray early morning (7:00 AM - 10:00 AM) or late afternoon (3:30 PM - 5:30 PM) to avoid pesticide photodegradation under strong midday sunlight (>35°C).

## Section 3: Severe Weather, Cyclone & Lodging Protection
- Cyclone / Heavy Storm Measures:
  - Ensure unhindered drainage in low-lying delta areas (e.g. Cauvery delta / Thanjavur / Nagapattinam).
  - If paddy is in dough/maturity stage and heavy storm is forecasted, harvest prematurely at 80% grain maturity to save the crop from submersion and viviparous germination.
  - Drain water completely 48 hours prior to severe storm landfall to strengthen root anchorage and reduce lodging susceptibility.
